UAE corporate tax and qualifying free zone notes

This file is a public briefing note. It is not a tax opinion, a return, or a determination by the Federal Tax Authority.

The schedule

Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 introduced federal corporate tax for financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023. The rate structure used on the MRV Associates website is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that amount. Confirm the current Cabinet threshold at https://tax.gov.ae/.

Qualifying Free Zone Persons

A Qualifying Free Zone Person may apply 0% to qualifying income where the statutory conditions are met, and 9% to taxable income that is not qualifying income. Public FTA material describes conditions that include adequate substance, qualifying activities, and a de minimis limit on non-qualifying revenue. That limit is generally described as the lower of 5% of total revenue and AED 5 million.

The website simulator

The simulator applies the 0% / 9% schedule, treats the qualifying-activity share as taxed at 0%, and applies the AED 375,000 band to the non-qualifying profit. At AED 5,000,000 of profit and a 75% qualifying rate, the illustration is AED 78,750 of tax, AED 416,250 of mainland tax, and AED 337,500 of relief. The verified de minimis state at those inputs also uses a 70% planning screen so the reference design stays visible. That screen is wider than the 5% test and is not an FTA clearance.
